## 🔹 1. Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

**Project Title:** Edu Genie – AI Powered Learning Assistant

**Problem Statement:**
Many students struggle to understand concepts clearly and face difficulties during exam preparation. Lack of personalized learning support reduces their learning efficiency.

**Idea Description:**
Edu Genie is an AI-based learning assistant that helps students understand topics easily, generates quizzes, and analyzes their performance.

**Target Users:**

* School Students
* College Students
* Competitive Exam Aspirants

**Expected Outcome:**

* Easy and interactive learning
* Better understanding of concepts
* Improved academic performance
